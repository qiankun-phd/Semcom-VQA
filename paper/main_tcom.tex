\begin{abstract}
Deploying edge-assisted Visual Question Answering (VQA) over resource-constrained wireless links requires balancing physical-layer transmission energy against server-side model inference complexity. Existing semantic communication (SemCom) frameworks predominantly presume analog, unquantized transmissions, employ toy-scale decoders, and overlook the energy consumption of large foundational Vision--Language Models (VLMs). This paper proposes an energy-frugal, cross-layer task-oriented semantic communication system for edge VQA under standardized digital physical layers. The system integrates deep variational neural image compression, 3GPP 5G-compliant $\text{LDPC}(1020, 512)$ channel coding over Rician fading ($K=6\text{ dB}$), and an edge-deployed Qwen3-VL-4B model adapted via parameter-efficient low-rank adaptation (LoRA). Under a rigorous dual-mode green energy formulation, we demonstrate that when transmitting under a constant query energy budget (Mode~2), selecting compact source bitrates unlocks up to $+3.01\text{ dB}$ and $+6.00\text{ dB}$ of physical SNR gain, shielding the link from catastrophic packet erasure. We develop a lightweight, channel-aware policy network that inspects multimodal query semantics and instantaneous physical channel state information (CSI) to dynamically co-optimize transmitter source bitrates ($2\text{k}, 4\text{k}, 8\text{k}$ bytes) and receiver visual token budgets ($T_v \in \{\mathrm{low}, \mathrm{medium}, \mathrm{high}\}$). Extensive evaluation over 2,400 independent blind test images across 10 channel SNR points ($-5.0\text{ dB} \dots 20.0\text{ dB}$) on the TDIUC benchmark demonstrates that our policy attains $36.92\%$ accuracy under harsh fading ($2.5\text{ dB}$), outperforming fixed 4k by $+29.92\%$ and channel-blind policies by $+27.71\%$. Furthermore, the policy autonomously prunes visual tokens by $49.2\%$ for low-entropy semantic tasks while strictly defining the non-dominated Pareto efficiency frontier across RF energy, system latency, and task accuracy.
\end{abstract}
