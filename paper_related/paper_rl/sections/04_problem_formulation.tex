\section{Problem Formulation}\label{sec:problem}

We model joint semantic-evidence and airspace-resource scheduling as a
constrained Markov decision process (CMDP)
$\langle\mathcal{S},\mathcal{A},P,r,\{c_i\},\{d_i\},\gamma\rangle$
\emph{with a budgeted escalation channel}. The certificate of
Section~\ref{subsec:cert} splits the query stream into the admitted set
$\mathcal{K}_{\mathrm{adm}}(\pi)$ (served, rejected-while-serveable, or
expired-while-serveable) and the escalated remainder; the rate constraints
are stated \emph{on the admitted set}, and escalation itself is a budgeted
event:
\begin{align}\label{eq:cmdp}
\max_{\pi}\;&\mathbb{E}\!\left[\sum_{t}\gamma^{t}\,r(s_t,a_t)\right]\\
\text{s.t.}\;\;
&\mathbb{E}\big[c_i \,\big|\, k\in\mathcal{K}_{\mathrm{adm}}(\pi)\big]\le d_i,
\;\; i\in\mathcal{I},\nonumber\\
&\mathbb{E}\big[\mathbf{1}\{k\ \text{escalated}\}\big]\le
\delta_{\mathrm{esc}},\nonumber
\end{align}
with $\mathcal{I}=\{$quality-n, quality-c, deadline-n, deadline-c, conflict,
battery, GPU$\}$. This admitted-set formulation is the honest one for an
overloaded safety system: a scheduler cannot promise a violation rate on
queries that are physically unserveable, but it can promise (i) a violation
rate on what it admits and (ii) a certified, budgeted escalation rate for
what it does not. Both promises are auditable, and Section~\ref{sec:experiments}
reports them channel-by-channel.

\subsection{State}
The recommended observation \texttt{state\_v2\_fixed} is
$\approx\!137$-dimensional and comprises: (i) a task block
(question-type one-hot, SNR, deadline, cache-hit rate, and the
spec-attainability certificate bit of Definition~\ref{def:cert}); (ii) per
evidence-level features (LUT payload and accuracy, plus seven
semantic-feasibility features per level---accuracy lower-confidence bound,
uncertainty, quality gap, joint feasibility); (iii) per-UAV state
($7$ dims: position, battery, status) and per-edge state ($4$ dims: CPU,
GPU, VRAM, queue); (iv) the water levels of five Lyapunov virtual queues;
and (v) mobility features and action-mask features stored for replay at
update time (Section~\ref{sec:algorithm}).

\subsection{Hybrid action space (two timescales)}
The action factorizes into a fast head, updated every slot, and a slow
head, updated every $K=3$ slots (Table~\ref{tab:action}). The fast head
carries the semantic-evidence routing and physical-resource shares; the
slow head carries task assignment and flight manoeuvres, whose effects
(delay, energy, conflict geometry) unfold over multiple slots. Continuous
sub-actions pass through a state-independent Gaussian, then a
sigmoid/tanh squash into their physical ranges. Two further outcomes
complete the action semantics: \emph{reject/defer}, produced when the
semantic feasibility projection (Section~\ref{sec:algorithm}) rewrites a
deadline-infeasible transmission into an explicit drop or postponement
(a first-class action with its own reward accounting), and
\emph{escalate}, which is \emph{not} policy-selectable---the certificate
of Definition~\ref{def:cert} adjudicates every critical/high query that
ends rejected or expired, so the policy cannot game the escalation channel
by dropping serveable safety queries.

\subsection{Constraint set}
The constraints are risk-differentiated: \emph{critical} tasks carry
tighter limits than \emph{normal} ones (Table~\ref{tab:constraints}). The
quality constraints cap the semantic-quality violation rate on the
admitted set; the deadline constraints cap the timeout-violation rate; the
conflict constraint caps the tactical conflict rate at the TLS-derived
budget of Section~\ref{sec:system}; battery and GPU constraints cap
resource-exhaustion risk; and the escalation budget caps how much of the
critical load may be handed upward.

\subsection{Reward and environment}
The reward is a heavily shaped \texttt{semantic\_utility} signal: semantic
success and an accuracy-lower-confidence-bound margin (risk-weighted, with
critical tasks scaled $\times1.6$), minus a quality-gap penalty,
delay/energy/payload costs, and manoeuvre/safety costs; a
cache-collapse penalty ($\sim\!-16\times$gap when $s_0$ is selected despite
a semantic gap) discourages degenerate reuse. The environment models a
six-term delay decomposition (fly$+$sense$+$tx$+$queue$+$infer$+$load),
a four-term energy model, GPU VRAM with an LRU model cache, the
fresh$\to$stale$\to$expired semantic cache, and the UTM/DSS conflict state
machine; the A2G propagation, shared-pool bandwidth share, and
slotted-serial uplink follow Section~\ref{subsec:a2g}, with received SNR
quantized to $\{-5,\dots,20\}$\,dB bins. This CMDP is the target of the
constrained-RL solver in Section~\ref{sec:algorithm}.
