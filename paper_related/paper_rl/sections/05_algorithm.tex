\section{Risk-Aware Constrained Hybrid-Action PPO\\ with Dual-Price Monitoring}
\label{sec:algorithm}

We name the method precisely: a \emph{risk-aware constrained hybrid-action
PPO with a certified escalation layer and dual-price diagnostics}, trained
on the two-timescale action space of Section~\ref{sec:problem} with a slow
mobility--semantic actor. The name reflects the division of labour
established in Section~\ref{sec:experiments}: in the feasible operating
regime the Lagrangian duals converge to interior shadow prices, and in the
infeasible overload regime the certificate-based escalation/admission
layer carries the safety guarantee while the dual's ceiling ratchet serves
as an infeasibility diagnostic. This section covers the network and
training (§\ref{sub:net}), the six-layer constraint machinery
(§\ref{sub:layers}), the correctness fixes and the ratio$\equiv$1 test
gate (§\ref{sub:fixes}).

\subsection{Network and training}\label{sub:net}
A shared MLP encoder ($128,128$; ReLU) feeds single-layer policy heads and
a value head; continuous heads use a state-independent learnable
$\log\sigma$. We optimize with Adam (lr $3\times10^{-4}$), PPO clip
$0.2$~\cite{schulman2017ppo}, and four epochs per episode.
Advantages use Monte-Carlo discounted returns minus the shared value
baseline (no GAE): episodes are short ($20$ steps) and rewards dense, so
GAE's bias--variance trade buys little and costs a hyperparameter; the
price is higher variance, noted as a limitation. Entropy is annealed
linearly $0.10\to0.02$ to suppress premature collapse to the cache action,
and a behaviour-cloning (BC) warm start from greedy demonstrations
is applied through a decaying auxiliary loss. Rollout collection is wrapped
in \texttt{no\_grad}. Training configuration is summarized in
Table~\ref{tab:train}.

\subsection{Six-layer constraint machinery}\label{sub:layers}
Constraint handling is realized by six stacked layers. The division of
labour, established empirically in Section~\ref{sec:experiments}, is that
the \emph{structural} layers (2--5) plus the \emph{admission} layer (6)
carry the safety load, while the \emph{dual} layer (1) prices the
constraints where they are feasible and diagnoses them where they are not.
\begin{enumerate}
\item \textbf{Lagrangian dual} (eight multipliers $\lambda_i$, incl.\ the
escalation channel): projected dual ascent
$\lambda_i\!\leftarrow\![(1{-}\kappa)\lambda_i+
\eta_i(c_i-d_i)]_{0}^{\lambda_i^{\max}}$
with risk-aware limits, ascent rates $\eta_i=0.1$ ($0.2$ for the conflict
channel), a leak $\kappa{=}0.01$ (releasing the ratchet so $\lambda$ can
decrease; disabled on the quality/escalation channels, which are pure
projected sub-gradients), per-channel ceilings
($\lambda^{\max}_{Q}{=}\lambda^{\max}_{\mathrm{conf}}{=}8$ vs.\ the global
$20$), and a $150$-episode dual warm-up during which ascent is frozen so
the policy forms under the BC/prior shaping before the constraint prices
start to move.
\item \textbf{Action mask}: hard feasibility masks (GPU-VRAM masks the
service level, battery masks the UAV, and manoeuvre modes are masked by
state).
\item \textbf{Semantic feasibility projection}: on a hard violation the
service is re-selected by a semantic safety score; a cache-override forces
an evidence upgrade under a large semantic gap, and a deadline-infeasible
transmission is rewritten into token$\to$cache$\to$reject$\to$defer, the
reject/defer outcomes of Section~\ref{sec:problem}. A structural
\emph{critical-cache ban} rules a critical/high query served from cache
alone never quality-compliant, closing the degenerate cache shortcut.
\item \textbf{Resource-floor projection}: per-route lower bounds on
bandwidth/power/CPU/GPU; resources are zeroed for $s_0$.
\item \textbf{Lyapunov virtual queues} ($\times5$): quality, deadline,
energy, risk, and UTM queues enter the observation and a drift-plus-penalty
term~\cite{chow2018lyapunov,neely2010stochastic}; updated incrementally.
\item \textbf{Certified escalation/admission} (Section~\ref{subsec:cert}):
the spec-attainability certificate adjudicates every critical/high query
that ends rejected or expired---attainable ones are charged as quality
violations, unattainable ones are escalated against the budget
$\delta_{\mathrm{esc}}$ through the dedicated dual channel. This is the
layer that \emph{creates} a feasible region in the overload regime
(Section~\ref{sec:experiments}).
\end{enumerate}

\begin{algorithm}[t]
\caption{Risk-aware constrained hybrid-action PPO with certified escalation}
\label{alg:main}
\footnotesize
\begin{algorithmic}[1]
\STATE Init.\ shared encoder, heads, value; $\lambda_i\!\gets\!0$; BC warm start.
\FOR{episode $=1,\dots,500$}
  \STATE Collect rollout ($20$ slots) under \texttt{no\_grad}; every slot run
         fast head, every $K{=}3$ slots run slow head; apply masks (L2),
         feasibility projection incl.\ reject/defer rewrite (L3), resource
         floors (L4).
  \STATE Certificate adjudication (L6): critical rejected/expired queries
         are charged (attainable) or escalated against
         $\delta_{\mathrm{esc}}$ (unattainable).
  \STATE Update Lyapunov queues (L5); log costs $c_i$ and multipliers $\lambda_i$.
  \FOR{epoch $=1,\dots,4$}
    \STATE Recompute log-probs; \textbf{assert} epoch-0 ratio$\equiv1$ (unit gate).
    \STATE PPO clip loss $+$ Lagrangian term $\sum_i\lambda_i c_i$ $+$
           Lyapunov drift $+$ decayed BC aux.
    \STATE Scale-only advantage normalization (preserve mean; keep $\lambda$ penalty).
  \ENDFOR
  \IF{episode $>150$ (dual warm-up over)}
    \STATE $\lambda_i\!\gets\!\mathrm{clip}\big((1{-}\kappa)\lambda_i+\eta_i(c_i-d_i),\,0,\,\lambda_i^{\max}\big)$
           per channel (L1).
  \ENDIF
\ENDFOR
\end{algorithmic}
\end{algorithm}

\subsection{Correctness audit and the ratio$\equiv$1 gate}\label{sub:fixes}
A two-model independent read-only audit (cross-validated, zero
contradictions) surfaced defects that had contaminated an earlier
``two-timescale vs.\ monolithic'' ablation. We enumerate the load-bearing
findings and their fixes.
\begin{itemize}
\item \textbf{Slow-head credit assignment (P0)}: the slow mobility head's
log-prob was counted every step ($\times K$), and its action mask was
applied at sampling but not at update, so the policy ratio $\neq1$ even at
epoch~0, spuriously triggering the PPO clip and mis-counting entropy.
\emph{Fix}: count the log-prob once per decision step (zero on
non-decision steps), store the mobility mask for replay, and multiply
entropy by the decision mask.
\item \textbf{Two-timescale mobility double-count (P0)}: the mobility cost
was already inside the \texttt{semantic\_utility} reward, and the
two-timescale path added it again, so the old structural ablation partly
measured a reward-definition difference. \emph{Fix}: remove the duplicate
adjustment and re-run.
\item \textbf{Multiplier ratchet (P1)}: with a zero limit and non-negative
cost, $\lambda$ was monotone non-decreasing---a barrier, not a shadow
price. \emph{Fix}: add the leak term and per-channel caps.
\item \textbf{Penalty double-counting (P1)}: a conflict set both an
airspace and a UTM flag, stacking a shaped penalty with the Lagrangian and
Lyapunov terms. \emph{Fix}: penalize the same event once; zero the UTM
channel weight; make queues incremental.
\item \textbf{Assigned-UAV CPA (P1-env)}: CPA used the nearest UAV, making
conflicts nearly exogenous. \emph{Fix}: use the assigned UAV's kinematics,
restoring policy controllability of conflict.
\item \textbf{BC lock-in / advantage normalization / efficiency (P1--P2)}:
decay the BC auxiliary weight; use scale-only advantage normalization so
the near-constant $\lambda$ penalty is not subtracted away; wrap rollout
collection in \texttt{no\_grad}.
\item \textbf{Reward-attribution wiring (P0, found post-audit)}: the
mission-aligned success attribution (a quality- and deadline-compliant
service that is merely UTM-blocked earns a discounted success bonus
instead of a penalty) was applied in the environment log but \emph{not} in
the learner's shaped reward, so the gradient never saw it. \emph{Fix}:
mirror the attribution into the controller reward; the unconstrained arm's
peak behaviour then improves measurably
(Section~\ref{sub:pathology}), which doubles as an end-to-end witness that
the reward path is load-bearing.
\end{itemize}
The standing regression gate is an \textbf{epoch-0 ratio$\equiv$1 unit
test}: immediately after collection we recompute the log-probs and assert
they equal the stored old log-probs step-by-step, guaranteeing the PPO
importance ratio starts at exactly $1$. The full suite passes $321$ tests,
including bit-for-bit legacy-regression guards on every constraint-machinery
lever (escalation, cache ban, dual caps, warm-up, reward attribution).
Per the audit, the results in Section~\ref{sec:experiments} are all on
the corrected system.
