\section{Conclusion}\label{sec:conclusion}

We presented a U-space/BUBBLES-conformant framework for jointly scheduling
semantic evidence levels and airspace resources in multi-UAV visual
question answering, in which every scenario parameter is traceable to a
SESAR JU deliverable and the separation kernel reproduces the official
numerics to within $0.18\%$. A corrected safety bridge couples the payload
to the tactical-contact separation chain through an M/G/1 priority queue:
tokens keep the separation service at its certified baseline, while peak
image traffic inflates the contact distance by $23.7$\,m and forfeits
$\approx\!6\%$ of relative airspace capacity---communication remains a
flight-safety variable, now with honest sign discipline. Scheduling is
formalized as a CMDP on the admitted set with a certificate-based,
budget-calibrated escalation channel, and solved by a risk-aware
constrained hybrid-action PPO behind a six-layer constraint stack. The
central empirical result is a dual-pathology contrast obtained by running
the same machinery in a feasible and an infeasible regime: under nominal
load the quality dual converges to an interior shadow price ($3.29$) and
the policy attains $93\%$ of the escalation-aware oracle's admitted
mission success; under peak overload the dual ratchets to its cap on
$3/3$ seeds, the constrained and unconstrained arms coincide, and safety
is carried structurally---the escalation-aware oracle reaches $0.916$ only
by escalating $0.666$ of the critical load, while the conflict, critical-
deadline, and escalation budgets all hold with margin. The methodological
takeaway---read a pinned multiplier as an infeasibility certificate and
reach for admission control rather than a higher price, verifying with
channel-off and warm-start-at-the-pin ablations---is the paper's most
portable contribution. Future work will close the nominal quality gap
that the interior dual is still pricing at episode $500$, learn the
escalate/serve split directly against the certificate budget, and pursue
a federated extension that shares evidence-selection statistics across a
U-space fleet while keeping the safety kernel local.
