\begin{abstract}
We propose \method, a multi-agent hybrid proximal policy optimization
algorithm that jointly optimizes the trajectory, transmit power,
channel, and semantic-symbol selection for multiple UAVs cooperatively
offloading visual question answering tasks to a multi-access edge
computing server. Coupling these decisions is critical because
DeepSC-VQA semantic similarity is non-monotonic in the symbol
cardinality and depends on signal-to-noise ratios that are themselves
shaped by the UAV trajectories, but prior single-UAV / static-channel
formulations leave this coupling unsolved under realistic dynamics.
\method casts the problem as a decentralized partially observable
Markov decision process whose joint action factorizes into a discrete
component (per-device channel and semantic-symbol selection) and a
continuous component (per-UAV trajectory and per-device transmit
power), trains the actor with a piecewise reward that ties resource
decisions to semantic-task fidelity, and inherits a monotonic
improvement guarantee under a clipped surrogate objective via an
additive KL decomposition across the two factors. Evaluation against
five baselines (MAPPO-hybrid, PADDPG, PDQN, single-UAV HPPO, and a
LUT-grade Greedy heuristic) on a measurement-grade DeepSC-VQA semantic
similarity look-up table demonstrates dominance across joint cost,
delay, energy, similarity, and success-rate axes, with robustness
preserved under multi-UAV scaling, channel and UE-count perturbations,
adversarial jamming up to 15\,dBm, and large-scale UE generalization to
$N{=}16$ without retraining. \method reduces the joint
delay--energy cost by 17\% relative to the strongest learning baseline
and lifts task success rate from $0.66$ to $0.81$ at $(M,N){=}(2,4)$.
\end{abstract}
