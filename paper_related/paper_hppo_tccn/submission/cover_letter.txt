Dear Editor,

We submit the manuscript "Multi-UAV Semantic Offloading for VQA Tasks
via Robust Hybrid Reinforcement Learning" for consideration as a
regular paper in IEEE TCCN.

This work substantially extends our conference paper "Hybrid
Reinforcement Learning for Resource Allocation in VQA-Oriented UAV
Semantic Offloading" with new formulation, a new algorithm, new theory,
and broader empirical evidence. The conference paper is cited in the
manuscript and is also enclosed for reference.

1. Substantial new contributions

  (i)   System model. The conference version assumed a single UAV
        serving three stationary users over a static air-to-ground
        channel. The journal extension models multi-UAV deployments
        (up to four UAVs and nine users) with Poisson task arrivals,
        random-walk user mobility, and per-slot Rician small-scale
        fading. The problem is reformulated as a Dec-POMDP with a
        unified delay--energy joint cost (Section III).

  (ii)  Algorithm. We replace single-agent HPPO with MA-HPPO, a
        multi-agent hybrid PPO under centralized training and
        decentralized execution. MA-HPPO uses role-aware specialized
        discrete heads (one per UAV / UE / channel slot) and a single
        Gaussian continuous head with a fixed standard deviation
        (Section IV). The conference single-agent HPPO is preserved
        as a baseline to demonstrate the multi-agent gain.

  (iii) Theoretical analysis. A new Section V derives two propositions
        for the factorized hybrid policy: an unbiased policy-gradient
        decomposition that separates the discrete and continuous
        score-function gradients, and a monotonic improvement bound
        under the clipped surrogate objective via additive KL
        decomposition. A per-iteration computational complexity
        analysis quantifies the trunk-encoder cost relative to the
        MAPPO-hybrid weight-shared variant.

  (iv)  Baselines. Three additional baselines accompany the conference
        PADDPG and PDQN: a parameter-shared MAPPO-hybrid variant, the
        conference single-UAV HPPO retained for backward-compatibility
        check, and a non-learning Greedy LUT heuristic that selects
        the best UE symbol cardinality by direct LUT look-up.

  (v)   Empirical evaluation. Five new experiment classes accompany
        the conference convergence / cost / similarity comparison:
        multi-UAV scaling on the (M,N) grid (Table II, Fig. 4); a
        four-axis ablation study (Table III); sensitivity to the
        delay--energy trade-off and per-channel bandwidth (Fig. 7);
        post-hoc robustness to channel SINR perturbation and to
        unseen UE counts (Fig. 8); adversarial jamming up to 15 dBm
        under a constant-power attacker (Fig. 9); and large-scale UE
        generalization to N = 16 without retraining (Table IV).

3. Reproducibility

  Code, configurations, and the experiment commit tag are released at
  https://github.com/qiankun-phd/hppo-uav (branch journal-ext, commit
  4dc7f94e). The DeepSC-VQA semantic-similarity look-up table file
  VQA_table.mat shipped with the env package has SHA-256
  6861b230eb97de885311698bb8b09830dc7886a773109bad75a637a3cbd93fe4
  for verification. The semantic similarity is loaded from this file
  via a deterministic 5x5 symbol grid by 7-bin SINR axis interpolation;
  see Section III.C for details.
